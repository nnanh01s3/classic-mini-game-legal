# Privacy Policy - Calm Blocks

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Calm Blocks ("the app") is a free puzzle game for Android published by **Classic Mini Game Studio** ("we", "us"). This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles. Contact: cedric.thientung@gmail.com

## 1. Summary
- No account, no sign-in, no purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
- Your game progress stays on your device.
- The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find crashes. These services process some technical data, in some regions only after you agree.
- We do not sell personal data.

## 2. Data stored on your device only
Your scores, saved game, daily streak and settings (sound, vibration, text size, language) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## 3. Data processed by Google services
The app includes Google SDKs. Google processes the data below under its own policies. We do not receive your advertising ID or your personal profile.

### Google AdMob (advertising)
To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process: your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language), your IP address (from which Google can estimate a coarse location), and ad interactions (such as views, taps and video views). Ads may be personalised or not, depending on your choices and where you live.

### Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (usage and stability)
To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, Firebase may process: app usage events (for example the start and end of a game and the mode played, score and duration), crash logs and basic device information at the time of a crash, and an app installation ID that does not identify you personally.

### Firebase Remote Config
The app downloads a few app-configuration settings from Firebase (for example how often ads may appear). This is necessary for the app to work as designed, so it runs for all users, right after the consent step on launch (including where no consent form is shown, or when the form could not be loaded), and is not controlled by the analytics choice. For this, Firebase processes a Firebase installation ID and basic information such as country, language, time zone and app and OS version.

## 4. Your choices and consent
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever Google's consent form is required), the app shows Google's consent form on first launch. Analytics and crash reporting are switched on only for the purposes you agree to, and ads are shown according to your choices.
- Where the form applies, an **Ad privacy choices** button in Settings lets you review or change your choices at any time.
- Elsewhere, no consent form is shown and these services are enabled by default. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

## 5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us.

## 6. Retention and sharing
We do not keep personal data ourselves. Google keeps the data it processes according to its policies:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with these Google services to operate ads, analytics and crash reporting. Data sent from the app is encrypted in transit.

## 7. Deleting your data
Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device. To ask about or request deletion of data held by us or by the services above, email cedric.thientung@gmail.com. You can also manage or delete Google ad-related data in your Google account settings.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept it.
